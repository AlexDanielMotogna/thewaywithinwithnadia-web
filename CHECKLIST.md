## Phase 10 — Pricing & invoices in the admin _(Nadia meeting 2026-10-16)_

**Golden rule: nothing is paid through the website or the app.** Clients pay outside it (bank
transfer etc.). The website shows no checkout, no payment provider, no card form. The tools
below exist only in `/admin`, for Nadia. Supersedes the "online payments" idea in Master Doc
§28 / ENGINEERING.md §5.6 / LEGAL.md §7.7 (those docs get updated when this phase starts).

- [ ] Pricing model per service/session: **per hour** or **fixed price for the whole session** (discriminated union `{ kind: 'hourly'; ratePerHour } | { kind: 'fixed'; amount }`, total computed in `domain/`, snapshot on the booking)
- [ ] Nadia can edit sessions from the admin at any time (through `transition()`/`rescheduleBooking`, not by editing raw fields)
- [ ] `modules/invoicing/` with an invoice **state machine**, same pattern as bookings: `draft` (Entwurf: free to edit/delete) → `issued` (Offen: frozen, gets its sequential Rechnungsnummer, PDF generated once and stored) → `paid` (Bezahlt: Nadia enters the payment date by hand). Plus `cancelled` for an issued invoice that was reversed by a credit note.
- [ ] Immutability enforced **by the database**, not only the UI: a Postgres trigger rejects `UPDATE`/`DELETE` on amounts, names, dates, line items and number once status ≠ `draft` (only `status` → `paid` + `paidAt` allowed). Integration test proves it. Same idea as the booking exclusion constraint.
- [ ] Sequential numbering with no gaps (`F-2026-045`): counter row locked inside the issue transaction (a Postgres `SEQUENCE` can leave gaps on rollback). Separate series for credit notes (`NC-2026-012`).
- [ ] "Cancel invoice with credit note" (Stornorechnung) button: creates a new document with its own number and negative amounts referencing the original, then lets Nadia create a fresh corrected invoice. No edit button on issued invoices.
- [ ] Three separate dates, clearly labelled in the admin: **Rechnungsdatum** (issue date, set automatically on issue), **Leistungsdatum** (service date or period, **required before issuing**, printed on the PDF), **Zahlungsdatum** (payment date, entered by hand later).
- [ ] Legal footer text on the PDF picked automatically, never typed by hand. **Note:** the Kleinunternehmer text ("Umsatzsteuerbefreit — Kleinunternehmer gemäß § 6 (1) Z 27 UStG") depends on **Nadia's** status as the issuer (a `BillingSettings` global), not on the client. Reverse charge ("Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers") depends on the **client** being a business in another EU country with a valid UID (a client/invoice option, UID required). Which combinations are valid → PENDING.md #26 **[accountant]**
- [ ] PDF download in the admin (generated server-side once on issue, stored in the media bucket, served as-is afterwards)
- [ ] Invoices kept 7 years (BAO): the retention job and the data-subject "delete by email" tool must **not** delete invoices (LEGAL.md §4.3.8/§4.3.9 need an exception). LEGAL.md §4.2, `docs/data-inventory.md` and the privacy policy get a new "Invoicing" row.
- [ ] FAGG still applies: a priced booking creates a payment obligation even when payment happens outside the site, so the booking button keeps "Zahlungspflichtig buchen" (LEGAL.md §7.2)

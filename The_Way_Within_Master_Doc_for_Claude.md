# MASTER BUILD DOCUMENT --- THE WAY WITHIN WITH NADIA

## 0. Purpose of this document

This is the single source of truth for designing and building the
website **The Way Within with Nadia**.

The website must not look like a generic wellness template. Its visual
identity must be derived primarily from the supplied **"Presentation -
Example"** reference, while the brand philosophy, messaging, services
and content direction come from the supplied **"Journey to Self
Introduction"** presentation and Nadia's written website brief.

The final product must feel **warm, sacred, elegant, Mediterranean,
calm, natural and premium**, while remaining technically modern, fast,
responsive, editable and conversion-focused.

# 3. Visual Design System

## Colour direction

Use a restrained natural palette inspired by the reference:

-   Warm ivory / cream --- primary page background.
-   Soft sand / beige --- secondary backgrounds and cards.
-   Deep olive green --- primary brand text, navigation and buttons.
-   Muted sage / olive --- secondary accents.
-   Warm antique gold --- fine lines, icons, dividers and premium
    accents.
-   Charcoal / near-black --- only where stronger readability is
    necessary.
-   White --- selectively, not as the dominant clinical background.

Avoid: - Bright saturated colours. - Cold blue UI. - Generic purple
wellness gradients. - Heavy black sections. - Excessive gold. - Loud
shadows. - Glossy "tech startup" styling.

## Typography

Use an elegant editorial combination:

1.  **Primary serif:** refined, premium, high-contrast or classical
    serif for major headings and brand moments.
2.  **Secondary sans-serif:** clean, highly readable typeface for
    navigation, body copy, forms and UI.
3.  **Script/accent font:** used sparingly for "with Nadia", selected
    quotes or emotional accents.

Do not use script for long paragraphs or functional UI.

## Layout

-   Large margins and breathing room.
-   Editorial layouts rather than dense grids.
-   Full-width cinematic imagery where appropriate.
-   Alternating text/image sections.
-   Soft section transitions.
-   Thin gold or olive dividers.
-   Rounded corners only if subtle.
-   Avoid excessive card-based UI.
-   Avoid making every section look like a SaaS component.

## Photography direction

Preferred imagery: - Mediterranean architecture. - Morning sunlight. -
Olive trees and greenery. - Candles. - Linen. - Natural stone. -
Meditation. - Conscious breathing. - Calm human presence. - Retreat
settings. - Spa / thermal resort environments. - Authentic portraits of
Nadia when supplied.

Images should feel warm, organic, editorial and real.

# 4. Responsive Design --- NON-NEGOTIABLE

The entire website must be fully responsive.

It must be designed and tested for:

-   Desktop.
-   Laptop.
-   Tablet.
-   Mobile.

Mobile is particularly important because a large percentage of
prospective clients are expected to arrive from their phones.

On mobile:

-   Navigation must be simple and elegant.
-   Hero copy must remain readable.
-   CTAs must be easy to tap.
-   Booking must be comfortable to complete.
-   Forms must be mobile-friendly.
-   No horizontal scrolling.
-   Images must crop intentionally.
-   Typography must scale responsively.
-   Spacing must remain premium rather than becoming cramped.
-   Music controls must remain accessible without obstructing content.
-   Language switcher must remain accessible.
-   Performance must remain strong on mobile connections.

Do not create desktop first and merely "shrink" it. Mobile should feel
intentionally designed.
